## Why it exists

"How would you build an AI feature?" is a classic closing question. One ordered answer stops rambling.

## How it works

<Steps label="The 30-second answer">
  <Step title="Expose an API">The app calls Spring Boot, never the provider ([integration](card:spring-ai-integration)).</Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate and authorise">Know user and tenant first.</Step>

## Interview Qs

### How would you build an AI feature?

Walk the six steps on one example, such as a refund assistant, then list the add-ons. Stress that the backend, not the model, authorises actions.

### In a payment app, should the LLM decide refunds?

No. It may explain a failed payment and request `refundPayment`; the backend checks user, amount and policy, and the payment service executes it.
