## Why it exists

The kind of data you have decides how a model can learn. Labelled answers, raw unlabelled data and a reward signal each need a different method.

## Interview Qs

### Supervised vs unsupervised learning?

Supervised learning trains on examples that carry the right answer (a label) and predicts it for new data. Unsupervised learning gets no labels and finds structure, such as clusters, that a person interprets later.

### Classification vs regression?

Both are supervised. Classification predicts a category, such as fraud or not fraud. Regression predicts a number, such as how many days a refund will take.

### Is LLM pre-training supervised or unsupervised?

Self-supervised: the model creates its own labels from raw text by predicting hidden or next tokens. No person labels the data. Supervised fine-tuning on labelled examples comes after.
