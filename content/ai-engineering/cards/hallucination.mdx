## Why it exists

The model writes likely text, not checked facts. Asked for a refund policy it never saw, it may invent "refunds arrive in 2 days" and sound sure.

## Interview Qs

### How do you reduce hallucinations?

Ground the model in retrieved sources, improve retrieval, let it say "I don't know", require citations, constrain and validate the output, evaluate on test cases, and add human review for high-risk answers.

### Why do LLMs hallucinate at all?

They predict plausible text, and training and benchmarks reward guessing over admitting uncertainty. Missing or poor context and stale knowledge make guessing more likely.
