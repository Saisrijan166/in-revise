## What it is

These are four words you will hear together, and they nest inside each other rather than compete. Artificial intelligence (AI) is the broad goal: any system that does tasks needing human-like reasoning, including plain hand-written rules. Machine learning (ML) is the part of AI that learns the rule from data instead of a person writing it. Deep learning is ML built from neural networks with many layers. Generative AI (GenAI), such as [an LLM](card:what-is-an-llm), is deep learning that creates new content.

## Why it exists

Interviewers and product teams use these four words loosely, and treating them as one thing leads to the wrong tool. If you reach for an LLM to score fraud, you pay more and get a less reliable answer than a small classic model would give. Knowing how the words nest tells you, for each problem, whether a rule, a classic model or an LLM fits.

1. Start with AI, the widest box: it covers anything from a fixed rule to a chatbot.
2. Step inside to ML: now a model learns its behaviour from example data, instead of a person coding every case.
3. Step inside again to deep learning: the model is a neural network with many layers, which learns its own features from raw data.
4. Step inside once more to GenAI: these deep-learning models produce new content (text, images, audio), and LLMs are the text kind.

So each inner box is a narrower, more powerful special case of the one around it.

What to notice:

- In the payments app, a fraud score on a refund request is discriminative: it maps features to one number.
- Drafting the customer reply is generative: the output is new language that did not exist before.
- An LLM is a generative foundation model for text; a multimodal model takes or makes more than one kind of content, such as text and images.

## Not to be confused with

A discriminative model answers "which label or number?" for an input, such as refund-or-not. A generative model answers "produce new content", such as the reply text. Rule: discriminative picks from what exists; generative creates something new.

## Interview Qs

### What is the difference between AI, ML and deep learning?

AI is the broad field of machines doing tasks that need reasoning, including fixed rules. ML is the part of AI that learns its behaviour from data. Deep learning is ML built from neural networks with many layers, which learn their own features.

### Discriminative vs generative model?

A discriminative model predicts a label or number from features, such as "refund ticket or not". A generative model creates new content, such as a drafted reply. LLMs are generative. Classic scoring and forecasting usually stay discriminative because they are cheaper and easier to measure.

### What is a foundation model?

A large model trained on broad data at scale that can then be adapted to many tasks, for example by prompting or fine-tuning. The term comes from a 2021 Stanford report. LLMs are the text-based foundation models.
