Interviewers use these four words loosely. Knowing how they nest tells you which tool fits a problem: a rule, a classic model, or an LLM.

## Interview Qs

### What is the difference between AI, ML and deep learning?

AI is the broad field of machines doing tasks that need reasoning. ML is the part of AI that learns from data. Deep learning is ML with neural networks of many layers.

### Discriminative vs generative model?

A discriminative model predicts a label from features, such as "refund ticket or not". A generative model creates new examples, such as a drafted reply. LLMs are generative.

### What is a foundation model?

A large model trained on broad data at scale that can be adapted to many tasks, for example by prompting or fine-tuning. The term comes from a 2021 Stanford report.
