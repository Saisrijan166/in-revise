## Why it exists

Apps on different nodes must find and reach each other, though Pods move and change IPs.

## How it works

<Mermaid
  alt="order-service asks CoreDNS for payment-service.payments and gets the ClusterIP. kube-proxy rules on its node turn that into one Pod IP, and the CNI network carries the packet to that Pod on another node."
  chart={`flowchart LR
  A[order-service Pod] -- "1 lookup payment-service.payments" --> D[CoreDNS]
  D -- "2 ClusterIP" --> A
  A -- "3 connect" --> K[kube-proxy rules]
  K -- "4 one Pod IP" --> N[CNI Pod network]
  N --> P[payment-service Pod, other node]`}
/>

- Some CNI plugins replace kube-proxy with their own proxy.
- A [headless Service](card:headless-service) name returns the Pod IPs, not one ClusterIP.
- Who may talk to whom is set by [NetworkPolicies](card:network-policies).

## Example

```text
# from namespace payments
payment-service                             -> 10.96.12.34 (example ClusterIP)
payment-service.payments                    -> 10.96.12.34
payment-service.payments.svc.cluster.local  -> 10.96.12.34 (full name)

# from namespace orders
payment-service                             -> not found (searches orders)
payment-service.payments                    -> 10.96.12.34
```

## Defaults

| What | Default | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kube-proxy mode on Linux | iptables | Kubernetes 1.37 |
| Pod `dnsPolicy` | ClusterFirst (use cluster DNS) | Kubernetes 1.37 |

## Interview Qs

### How does a Pod on one node reach a Pod on another node?

Directly by Pod IP: the CNI plugin links nodes without NAT. Via a Service, kube-proxy (or the CNI's proxy) first turns the ClusterIP into one Pod IP.

### Why does `payment-service` resolve in one namespace but not in another?

A short name is looked up only in the caller's namespace. From elsewhere, add the namespace: `payment-service.payments`.

### What does a headless Service return in DNS?

The IPs of its Pods, not a ClusterIP. A StatefulSet's Pods also get their own names: `payments-db-0.payments-db.payments.svc.cluster.local`.
