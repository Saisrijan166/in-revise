## Why it exists

In [cache-aside](card:cache-aside) every service repeats the load-and-store code, and writes leave the cache briefly stale. A cache layer that loads and writes for you trades that for control or durability.

## How it works

<Compare id="cache-aside-vs-read-through-vs-write-through-vs-write-behind" />

- Write-behind merges repeated writes to one entry and batches them, so the database sees fewer writes.
- Until its queue is flushed, the cache holds the only copy: keep payment records out of write-behind.

## Interview Qs

### Why pair write-through with lazy loading?

Write-through fills the cache only on writes, so a new or restarted node lacks data until it is written again. Lazy loading fills those gaps on reads, and a TTL clears data nobody reads.

### When would you choose write-behind?

When writes are heavy and the database is the bottleneck, and losing or reordering the last few writes is acceptable. Database writes must not fail later, because the caller already got a success.
