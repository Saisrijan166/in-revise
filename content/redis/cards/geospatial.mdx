## Why it exists

"Show payment kiosks near me" needs a distance search. Redis answers it in memory, without a separate geo database.

## How it works

<Mermaid
  alt="GEOADD takes a longitude, a latitude and a member name. Redis interleaves the bits of the two coordinates into a 52-bit geohash and stores it as the member's score in a sorted set. GEOSEARCH then reads nearby score ranges to return members within a radius or box."
  chart={`flowchart LR
  A["GEOADD lon lat member"] --> H[52-bit geohash]
  H --> Z[(sorted set score)]
  Z --> S["GEOSEARCH BYRADIUS 5 km"]`}
/>

## Example

```text
GEOADD kiosks 77.5946 12.9716 kiosk:mg-road 77.6408 12.9784 kiosk:indiranagar  -- 2
GEOSEARCH kiosks FROMLONLAT 77.60 12.97 BYRADIUS 5 km ASC WITHDIST
GEODIST kiosks kiosk:mg-road kiosk:indiranagar km  -- about 5.07 km
```

## Interview Qs

### How would you find the nearest merchants to a user?

GEOADD every merchant once. On a request, GEOSEARCH from the user's longitude and latitude BYRADIUS, sorted ASC with a COUNT limit, and add WITHDIST to show the distance.

### What are the limits of Redis geo?

Points near the poles (beyond about 85.05 degrees latitude) cannot be indexed. Distances assume a perfect sphere, so they can be off by up to 0.5%. Queries are radius or box only.
