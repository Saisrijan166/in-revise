## Why it exists

Routers need a destination they can find. A prefix lets them forward by network, not per host; private ranges plus NAT stretch the short IPv4 supply.

## Interview Qs

### Public vs private IP?

A public address is globally unique and reachable on the Internet. A private RFC 1918 address is reused inside many networks and is unreachable from outside without NAT or a proxy.

### What does 10.0.0.0/24 mean?

The first 24 bits are the network: 10.0.0.0 to 10.0.0.255, 256 addresses. Classic subnets keep .0 (network) and .255 (broadcast), leaving 254 hosts; AWS reserves 5, leaving 251.

### What is NAT and why is it used?

The router rewrites the private source IP and port to its public IP and a port it picks, and reverses it for replies. Many hosts share one IPv4 address; outsiders cannot connect in without port forwarding.
