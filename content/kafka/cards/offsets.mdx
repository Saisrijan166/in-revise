## Why it exists

A consumer must know where it stopped. Kafka numbers each record within its partition, so progress is just one number per partition, not one acknowledgement per record.

## How it works

| Marker in orders-0 | Means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Log-start offset | First record still kept | 0 |
| Committed offset | Where the group restarts after a crash | 3 |
| Position | Next record poll() returns | 5 |
| High watermark | End of fully replicated records; reads stop here | 7 |
| Log-end offset | Where the next write lands | 8 |

- Saving the position: [offset commits](card:offset-commits); log-end minus committed is [consumer lag](card:consumer-lag).
- Moving the position back: [replay](card:replay).
- read_committed consumers stop earlier, before open [transactions](card:kafka-transactions).

## Interview Qs

### Is an offset unique across a topic?

No. Every partition counts from its own start, so offset 5 exists in orders-0 and orders-1. Topic, partition and offset together identify one record.

### A consumer is at position 5. What has it read?

Records 0 to 4 of that partition; it gets 5 next. If 5 was compacted away or is a transaction marker, it gets the next higher offset instead.
