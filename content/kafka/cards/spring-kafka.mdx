## Why it exists

The plain clients leave the poll loop, threads, commits and retries to you. Spring for Apache Kafka handles them; Spring Boot configures it from spring.kafka.* properties.

## How it works

## Defaults

| What | Default | Where |
|---|---|---|
| AckMode | BATCH: commit after all records of one poll are processed | Spring for Apache Kafka 4.1 |
| DefaultErrorHandler | FixedBackOff(0, 9): 10 deliveries, no delay, then log | Spring for Apache Kafka 4.1 |
| Dead letter topic | topic plus -dlt (.DLT before 3.3), same partition | DeadLetterPublishingRecoverer |

## Interview Qs

### What happens when a @KafkaListener method throws?

DefaultErrorHandler redelivers it (10 deliveries in all by default), then calls the recoverer: it logs, or DeadLetterPublishingRecoverer publishes the record to payments-dlt.

### How do you commit offsets manually in Spring?

Set AckMode to MANUAL or MANUAL_IMMEDIATE, add an Acknowledgment parameter to the listener method, and call acknowledge() after the work is done.
