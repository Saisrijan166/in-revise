## Why it exists

One log on one machine caps how much a topic can store and how fast it can be read. Partitions let many brokers store a topic and many consumers read it at once.

## Interview Qs

### What is a partition, and why split a topic?

A partition is one ordered, append-only log of a topic. Splitting spreads storage and load over brokers and lets several consumers in a group read in parallel, one partition each.

### Where does a partition live on disk?

As a folder named after the topic and partition number, such as orders-0, holding segment files. The leader and each follower replica keep their own copy on their broker.
