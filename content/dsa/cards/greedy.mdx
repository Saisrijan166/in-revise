## Why it exists

It gives a repeatable way to solve a common shape without trying every possibility from scratch.

## How it works

- Define the state that represents progress.
- Update it while preserving the invariant.
- Stop only when the answer condition is proved.

## Interview Qs

### What makes greedy valid?

A proof, not intuition: show a local choice can be part of an optimal answer.

### What should you explain while coding?

State the invariant, why each update preserves it, and the time and space complexity. Then dry-run a normal and boundary case.
