## Why it exists

Brute force recomputes overlapping subarrays. A window retains the state shared by neighbouring contiguous ranges.

## How it works

<Steps label="Variable window">
  <Step title="Expand">Add `s.charAt(right)` to the state.</Step>
  <Step title="Repair">While invalid, remove `s.charAt(left++)`.</Step>
  <Step title="Record">After repair, update the best valid answer.</Step>
</Steps>

- This needs a condition that can be maintained as ends move.

## Interview Qs

### Fixed versus variable sliding window?

A fixed window always has length k: add one item and remove one. A variable window grows right and shrinks left only while its condition, such as too many distinct values, is invalid.

### Why is a sliding window often O(n)?

Although there is a nested `while`, both pointers only move forward. Each item enters once through the right edge and leaves at most once through the left edge.
