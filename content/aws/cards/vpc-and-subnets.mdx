## Why it exists

Servers need private addresses, a split between public and internal parts, and control over where traffic goes, isolated from other AWS customers.

## How it works

- **Isolated** subnet: no routes outside the VPC; right for the database.

## Interview Qs

### What makes a subnet public?

Its route table sends traffic, usually 0.0.0.0/0, to an internet gateway. Each resource also needs a public IP and rules that allow the traffic.

### Why put subnets in two AZs?

A subnet lives in one AZ and fails with it. Give each tier subnets in two or more AZs ([multi-AZ design](card:regions-and-azs)).

### VPC peering or Transit Gateway?

Peering links two VPCs; it is not transitive and CIDRs must not overlap. For many VPCs or on-premises links, use a Transit Gateway as one Regional hub.
