## Why it exists

A fixed fleet is too small at peak, wasted at night, and a crashed server stays down. An Auto Scaling group sizes and heals the fleet.

## Interview Qs

### Traffic suddenly doubles. How does your design cope?

The ALB spreads requests over healthy instances; target tracking adds instances when CPU or requests per target pass the target, up to max. For known daily peaks, scheduled or predictive scaling adds capacity first.

### Target tracking or step scaling?

Target tracking: you set a target value; Auto Scaling creates the alarms and adjusts capacity in proportion, like a thermostat. Step scaling: you define alarms and how much to add per breach size. AWS recommends target tracking for metrics that move with capacity.
