## Why it exists

Users type a name, not an IP, and load balancer IPs change. DNS must also steer users away from a failed endpoint or to a nearer Region.

## Interview Qs

### Which routing policies does Route 53 support?

Simple, weighted, latency, failover, geolocation, geoproximity, IP-based and multivalue answer. Weighted suits canary splits, latency suits many Regions, failover suits active-passive.

### You changed a record, but some users still reach the old IP. Why?

Resolvers keep the old answer until its TTL runs out. Before changing a live record, lower its TTL, for example to 300 seconds, and raise it again once the change works.
