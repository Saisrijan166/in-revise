## Why it exists

"Design an order system with microservices" tests whether you can connect the concepts. A fixed order keeps the answer complete and calm.

## How it works

<Steps label="The answer, step by step, for an online shop">
  <Step title="Requirements">Flows, scale, and what must be correct at once (payment) or later (email).</Step>
  <Step title="Boundaries">Order, Payment, Inventory, Notification by [business capability](card:service-boundaries).</Step>
  <Step title="Calls">[Sync or async](card:sync-and-async-communication) per call; [gateway](card:api-gateway) at the edge.</Step>
  <Step title="Data">[A database per service](card:database-per-service); no shared tables.</Step>
  <Step title="Failures">[Timeouts and retries](card:timeouts-and-retries), [circuit breaker](card:circuit-breaker), [fallbacks](card:fallback-and-degradation).</Step>
  <Step title="Consistency">[Saga](card:saga), [outbox](card:outbox-and-inbox), [idempotency keys](card:idempotency).</Step>
  <Step title="Security">[Auth at the gateway, checks in every service](card:microservices-security).</Step>
  <Step title="Observability">[Traces, logs, metrics](card:observability) and [SLOs](card:metrics-and-slos).</Step>
  <Step title="Deploy and scale">[Kubernetes](card:containers-and-kubernetes), [probes](card:health-probes), [canary](card:deployment-strategies), [more instances](card:scaling-services).</Step>
</Steps>

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| "Microservices are faster." | They add network hops; they buy [independent deploys](card:monolith-and-microservices). |
| "Expose each service to clients." | Put an [API gateway](card:api-gateway) in front. |
| "@Transactional covers the payment call." | A local transaction cannot span services: [saga](card:saga) with compensations. |
| "The outbox gives exactly-once." | The relay can publish twice; [consumers stay idempotent](card:delivery-guarantees). |

## Interview Qs

### Can two services share a code library?

Yes, for stable technical code such as data access or messaging helpers. Do not share domain logic or entities: one change then forces both teams to release together.

### How would you design an order, payment and inventory system?

Walk the steps above in order, one line each, then go deep where money and stock meet: the saga, the outbox and idempotent payment. Say each trade-off aloud.
