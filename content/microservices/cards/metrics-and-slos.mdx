## Why it exists

"Is the shop healthy?" needs a number everyone agrees on. SLOs turn user experience into targets, and the error budget tells you when to stop shipping and fix reliability.

## How it works

<Compare id="sli-vs-slo-vs-sla" />

- USE suits resources such as CPU and disks: utilisation, saturation, errors.
- Record latency as histogram buckets, then compute p99 over all instances from the summed buckets.
- Track error latency separately: fast 500s make the overall latency look better than it is.

## Example

```text
SLO:          99.9% of checkout requests succeed, 30-day window
Error budget: 0.1% of requests
Traffic:      1,000,000 requests in 30 days  -> 1,000 may fail
As time:      0.1% of 30 days = 43.2 minutes of full outage
```

## Interview Qs

### What are the four golden signals?

Latency (time per request, success and failure apart), traffic (demand, such as requests per second), errors (failed, wrong or too-slow responses) and saturation (how full the tightest resource is).

### What happens when the error budget runs out?

While budget remains, releases go out. Once it is spent, releases pause for a while and the team puts the effort into testing, resilience and performance instead of features.
