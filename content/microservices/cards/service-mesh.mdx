## Why it exists

With dozens of services in several languages, coding mTLS, retries and metrics into each one is slow and uneven. A mesh does it once, below the app.

## Interview Qs

### What is the sidecar pattern?

A helper process or container deployed beside each app instance, sharing its life cycle. In a mesh, the sidecar is the proxy that all the instance's traffic passes through.

### When is a service mesh not worth it?

For a few services or a small app, a proxy per instance costs more than it gives. Very chatty, latency-sensitive calls also suffer from the extra hop.
