## Why it exists

Retries cannot hide every failure. A shop should still take orders when recommendations are down, instead of showing an error page.

## How it works

| Shop feature | Its dependency fails | Degrade to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recommendations | Recommendation service | Hide the section, or show a fixed list |
| Product page | Review service | Show the page without reviews |
| Order confirmation email | Notification service | Queue it; send when it is back |
| Payment | Payment service | A clear error or "payment pending", never "paid" |

- [Retry](card:timeouts-and-retries) first; fall back when retries run out or the [breaker](card:circuit-breaker) is open.
- Alert when many instances serve degraded answers, so nobody mistakes degraded for healthy.

## Interview Qs

### Retry vs fallback?

Retry repeats the same call, hoping a brief fault has passed. Fallback gives a different answer, such as cached or default data, once the call cannot succeed.

### When should you avoid a fallback?

When a wrong answer costs more than an error, as with payments and other transactions. Also when the fallback adds load, such as every server hitting the database once the cache fails.
