## Why it exists

Interviewers ask "where have you seen this pattern?". Naming a real JDK or Spring class shows you understand the pattern, not just its definition.

## Interview Qs

### Which design patterns does Spring use?

Factory (BeanFactory, FactoryBean), Singleton (the default bean scope), Proxy (AOP and @Transactional), Observer (application events), Front Controller (DispatcherServlet), Adapter (HandlerAdapter) and Builder (RestClient.builder()).

### Which patterns can you point to in the JDK?

Iterator (java.util.Iterator), Decorator (java.io streams), Adapter (InputStreamReader), Strategy (Comparator), Proxy (java.lang.reflect.Proxy), Flyweight (the Integer.valueOf cache) and Singleton (Runtime.getRuntime()).
