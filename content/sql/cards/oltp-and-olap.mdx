## Why it exists

The app needs quick single-row changes; managers need totals over millions of rows. Running both on one database slows the app.

## How it works

<Compare id="oltp-vs-olap" />

- PostgreSQL and MySQL InnoDB store whole rows in each page: row stores, built for OLTP.
- A warehouse keeps history, so its fact tables grow while dimensions stay small.

## Example

```sql
-- star schema: revenue per year and category
SELECT d.year, p.category, SUM(f.amount) AS revenue
FROM   sales_fact  f
JOIN   date_dim    d ON d.date_key    = f.date_key
JOIN   product_dim p ON p.product_key = f.product_key
GROUP  BY d.year, p.category;
```

## Interview Qs

### What is a star schema?

A fact table in the middle holds events with numeric measures, such as sales amounts, plus keys to dimension tables. Dimension tables, such as date, product and store, describe those events and are used to filter and group.

### What is the difference between ETL and ELT?

Both move data from sources into an analytics store. ETL transforms it in a separate engine before loading. ELT loads raw data first and transforms it inside the target store.

### Why not run analytics on the OLTP database?

Large aggregates scan many rows, use a lot of resources and can block business transactions. Highly normalised tables also make report queries long, with many joins.
